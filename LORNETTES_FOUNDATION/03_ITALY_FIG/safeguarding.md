## Problem

Under Italian sports regulations (Decreto Legislativo 36/2021) and Federazione Italiana Golf mandates, sports organizations working with minors must designate a Safeguarding Officer (Responsabile contro abusi, violenze e discriminazioni) and maintain explicit child-protection reporting protocols.

## Why It Matters

Institutional partnership with Italian federation academies cannot proceed without aligning with official Italian safeguarding frameworks. Non-compliance risks legal liability and immediate disqualification from federation procurement.
