## IMPORTANT NOTICE

> **THIS IS NOT PART OF CLUB MVP OR GOLF V1.1.**
>
> Requirements in this workstream are strictly decoupled from Canadian and US private-club MVP development. They exist as an institutional research repository to track potential European and Italian Golf Federation (Federazione Italiana Golf: FIG) requirements without distracting from immediate product goals.

Italy and FIG work starts only when:
1. A verified, meaningful Italian institutional opportunity or pilot agreement exists.
2. Requirements are officially confirmed with federation leadership or verified local legal counsel.
3. Privacy, safeguarding, and data-residency assumptions are legally validated.
4. Scope and budget are explicitly authorized by product leadership.
