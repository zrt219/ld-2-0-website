## Problem

Delivery of Lornette's Foundation within the Italian Golf Federation (FIG) or Italian junior academies requires accurate, culturally nuanced Italian translation across the platform interface, lesson captions, workbook PDFs, and field tools.

## Why It Matters

Golf terminology in Italy blends traditional English terms (e.g. birdie, bogey, handicap) with Italian athletic phrasing. Literal machine translation of psychological concepts or golf terminology creates confusion and undermines athlete engagement.

## Proposed Direction

If institutional expansion into Italy is formally approved, conduct a structured localization project:
1. Translate core platform UI strings, prompts, and navigation elements to standard Italian (`it-IT`).
2. Professional translation of all 10 Foundation lesson subtitles and video transcripts.
3. Translation and localized layout for participant field tools and the 5-Second Mistake Reset card.
4. Human linguistic review conducted by bilingual Italian golf professionals to verify authentic athletic tone.
5. Audio localization or high-quality Italian voiceover evaluation.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Complete translation dictionary compiled for golf and mental performance terminology
- [ ] Interface strings extracted and managed via Next.js internationalization framework
- [ ] Certified Italian human translator with golf domain experience signs off on copy
- [ ] Italian video captions time-synced and verified for all video assets
- [ ] Field tools and PDF downloads rendered in clean, culturally aligned Italian

## Non-Goals

- Implementing i18n infrastructure or Italian strings during Club MVP
- Machine-translating content without certified human review

## Evidence Needed

- Formal contract or pilot agreement with Federazione Italiana Golf or an Italian golf club
- Retainer of qualified Italian localization and golf terminology specialist
