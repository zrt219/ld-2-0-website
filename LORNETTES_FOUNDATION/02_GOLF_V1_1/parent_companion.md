## Problem

Parents of junior tournament golfers often experience intense emotional investment. On the drive home after a tough round or missed cut, well-meaning questions like "Why did you three-putt 17?" or "What was your score on the front nine?" can dismantle hours of composure training and trigger conflict.

## Why It Matters

Foundation 7 emphasizes "Family & Community Support". Parents are the primary emotional sanctuary for young golfers. If parents lack guidance on how to support their child after a difficult competition, the junior golfer often burns out or develops severe performance anxiety.

## Proposed Direction

Develop a lightweight Junior Parent Weekly Companion providing concise, practical guidance aligned to each week's Foundation:
- Philosophy: Support athlete development without turning the parent into another coach or critic.
- Core Theme: The car ride home after a tournament. What to say, what not to say, and how to create an unconditional emotional sanctuary.
- Format: One concise, high-impact weekly summary note (available via downloadable PDF or parent-facing dashboard view).
- Delivery: Zero automated email sending. Outbound emails strictly require the user's separate two-step authorization. Primary delivery should be portal-based or club-downloadable.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] 10-week Parent Companion content written with Lornette's Olympian lens
- [ ] Explicit guidance on post-round conversations and emotional boundary setting
- [ ] Athlete privacy preserved: parents cannot view junior reflection diary entries
- [ ] Safe delivery model: web portal or downloadable PDF; zero automated email dispatch
- [ ] Tested with junior golf parents and club junior golf committee chairs

## Non-Goals

- Creating a heavy parental surveillance portal or tracking software
- Permitting parents to edit, approve, or audit athlete performance plans
- Sending unauthorized automated marketing or update emails
