## Problem

Golfers competing in high school matches, club championships, collegiate invitationals, and national match-play qualifiers encounter unique psychological pressures that stroke play does not replicate. Direct opponent gamesmanship, conceding putts, dormie pressure, and alternate-shot team dynamics require acute composure drills that current generic stroke-play prompts lack.

## Why It Matters

Match play is won or lost on momentum management and emotional stamina. If an athlete lets an opponent's pace or lucky bounce disrupt their focus on the 14th green, the match ends immediately. Training decision-making under match-play conditions bridges theory and tournament reality.

## Proposed Direction

Develop a curated Match Play Scenario Bank mapped into the existing 10 Athletic Foundations and Performance Edge tools.
Candidate Scenarios:
1. **Dormie Pressure (3 Down with 3 to Play)**: Grounding focus when elimination is imminent; executing one shot without scoreboard math. (Maps to Foundation 4: Resilience After Setback)
2. **The Must-Hole 6-Foot Putt to Extend**: Calming heart rate, adhering to full pre-shot cadence, isolating sensory cues. (Maps to Foundation 5: Pressure, Emotional Regulation & Recovery)
3. **Opponent Gamesmanship & Needle**: Intentional walking delays, lingering in vision lines, commentating on club choice. (Maps to Foundation 2: Champion Mindset)
4. **Slow Play & Fairway Stalls**: Maintaining athletic warmth and mental engagement during 20-minute group backups. (Maps to Foundation 3: Discipline Systems)
5. **Conceding and Being Conceded Putts**: Managing expectations and remaining sharp after early "gimmes" stop on hole 16. (Maps to Foundation 6: Communication & Presence)
6. **Foursomes / Alternate Shot Burden**: Carrying emotional responsibility when a teammate hits a drive out of bounds. (Maps to Foundation 7: Family & Community Support)
7. **Fourball Partner Collapse**: Stepping up when your partner is out of the hole without playing defensively. (Maps to Foundation 1: Identity Beyond Sport)
8. **Sudden-Death Playoff**: First tee box crowd pressure, narrowing visual target, committing to the aggressive line. (Maps to Foundation 10: Legacy & Community Impact)
9. **National or Team Championship Pressure**: Representing your state, province, or collegiate team where overall victory hinges on your individual match outcome. (Maps to Foundation 9: Personal Brand & Story)

Important: Scenarios do not alter the 10 Foundations. They provide practical, sports-specific contextual application for weekly lessons.
